Meta Developer Setup Guide

Zero Ban Risk • 100% Free Official Setup

1. Link Instagram to a Facebook Page ▼
1. Open your Instagram mobile app.
2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Account Type & Tools > Switch to Professional Account (Creator or Business).
3. Go to Edit Profile > Public Business Information > Page and link to any Facebook Page you own (create a free page if needed).
2. Create a Free Meta App ▼
1. Visit developers.facebook.com and sign in.
2. Click My Apps > Create App.
3. Select Other > choose Business as the app type.
4. Enter an App Name (e.g. My Instagram Automator) and complete creation.
5. Copy your App ID and App Secret into the form on the left.
3. Add Instagram & Messenger Products ▼
1. In your Meta App Dashboard, click Add Product.
2. Find and add Instagram Graph API.
3. Also add Messenger product to the app.
4. Generate Page Access Token ▼
1. In the left menu, click Tools > Graph API Explorer.
2. In Meta App, select your newly created app.
3. In User or Page, select your Facebook Page.
4. Add the permissions:
  • instagram_manage_messages
  • instagram_manage_comments
  • pages_manage_metadata
  • pages_read_engagement
5. Click Generate Access Token and paste it into the left form.
5. Expose Webhook URL (ngrok) ▼
If developing locally on your PC, expose port 8000 via ngrok or Cloudflare Tunnel:
ngrok http 8000
Copy the resulting https://....ngrok-free.app into the "Public Webhook Base URL" field on the left.
6. Configure Webhook in Meta ▼
1. In Meta Developer Dashboard, click Webhooks in the left sidebar.
2. Under the dropdown, select Instagram.
3. Click Edit Subscription / Add Callback URL.
4. Paste your Callback URL: https://your-tunnel.ngrok-free.app/webhook
5. Enter your Verify Token: my_secure_custom_verify_token_123
6. Click Verify and Save.
7. Subscribe to the messages and comments fields. Done! 🎉
