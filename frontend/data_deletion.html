User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform Rules, repli-auto provides explicit instructions on how users can request the deletion of their interaction data.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you have interacted with our Instagram automated messaging system and wish to remove your data, you can do so through any of the following methods:

Method 1: Direct Message Command (Instant)

Send a direct message on Instagram to our connected page containing the single keyword: DELETE MY DATA. Our system will immediately purge all interaction logs and stored IDs associated with your account.

Method 2: Remove via Facebook / Instagram Account Settings
  1. Go to your Facebook or Instagram profile Settings & Privacy.
  2. Navigate to Apps and Websites or Website permissions.
  3. Find repli-auto and click Remove.
  4. Check the box to request that Meta notify us to delete your activities.

Confirmation of Deletion

Once requested, your scoped user ID and associated message delivery logs are permanently purged from our local database within 24 hours.
