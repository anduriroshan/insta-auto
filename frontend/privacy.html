Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how repli-auto ("we", "our", or "us") processes information when you interact with our Instagram messaging automation services.

1. Information We Collect

When you send a direct message or comment on our Instagram account, we receive information provided via the official Meta Graph API:

  • Your Instagram Scoped User ID (an anonymized identifier assigned by Meta)
  • Your public Instagram handle / username
  • The content of your direct message or comment to match automated keyword triggers

2. How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Deliver automated responses to requested keywords (such as resources, guides, or links)
  • Prevent duplicate replies via cooldown timers
  • Log delivery status to verify successful communication

3. Data Retention & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. Data is stored securely in our private self-hosted database and is never shared with third parties or external marketing networks.

4. Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to have your interaction logs deleted from our system, please review our Data Deletion Policy or send a DM with the word DELETE to have your records erased automatically.

5. Contact Us

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact the administrator directly through our Instagram page.
